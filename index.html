I am an economist in the Research Department at the Federal Reserve Bank of Boston. My research focuses on labor economics, particularly employment dynamics, skills and jobs, and economic inequality.

I received a joint PhD in Economics and Social Work from the University of Michigan, where I also served as a postdoctoral fellow at Poverty Solutions prior to joining the Boston Fed.
